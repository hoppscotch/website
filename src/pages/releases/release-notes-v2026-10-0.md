## Release Notes - v2026.10.0: Enterprise AI, Desktop Sign-In, and more

Hoppscotch v2026.10.0 brings the AI Assistant to Enterprise organizations and makes desktop workflows work better with organizational identity providers and trusted certificates. 🚀

<TypeBadge type="new features"/>

<BannerImage imageSource="/images/release-2026-10-0.png"/>

<br />

### Highlights

- **AI Assistant for Enterprise organizations:** Enterprise org members can use the AI Assistant to help with API work directly in Hoppscotch.

<br /><br />

- **Browser sign-in on desktop:** Sign in to Enterprise organizations through your default browser, supporting your organization's authentication flow and returning to the desktop app when complete.

<br /><br />

- **System trust store support:** The desktop app can use certificates trusted by your operating system, helping it work with organization-managed certificates and internal services.
